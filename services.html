what we deliver

our structured trade divisions

We operate through two focused departments: the bulk export of natural resources and strategic end-to-end procurement solutions for enterprise clients.

division 01

Export Division

Bulk direct sourcing, quality inspection, and maritime shipping logistics of primary agricultural products and solid minerals from Nigeria to global processing centers.

Raw Agricultural Commodities

Sourced from verified cooperative farming networks under strict post-harvest storage standards to preserve freshness and oil content.

  • Cocoa Beans (Raw, dry beans for confectioners)
  • Cashew Nuts (High-yield, raw-in-shell cashews)
  • Sesame Seeds (Premium white and brown seeds)
  • Ginger Root (Sun-dried split ginger, highly pungent)
  • Hibiscus Flower (Dried calyces for herbal infusions)
  • Shea Butter (Refined and unrefined cosmetic grades)

Solid Mineral Ores

Direct trade arrangements from vetted mine site operations. All shipments are backed by independent lab assay certifications prior to port loading.

  • Lithium Ore (High concentration spodumene/lepidolite)
  • Tantalite (Raw columbite-tantalite ore)
  • Lead & Zinc Concentrate (Industrial grade metal ores)
  • Limestone (High calcium carbonate content)
workflow framework

our export delivery sequence

Our structured end-to-end processing pipeline guarantees quality validation at every phase.

01

Sourcing & Procurement

Direct collection from primary producer cooperatives and mine gates under our direct agent oversight.

02

Quality Control & Assay

Rigorous processing, sorting, moisture checks, and certified third-party laboratory analysis to guarantee spec compliance.

03

Regulatory Documentation

Complete execution of customs entries, NEPC export declarations, NXP forms, and phytosanitary licensing.

04

Logistics & Port Freight

Secure container transport to major shipping terminals (Apapa / Tin Can Island) under cargo tracking protocols.

05

Maritime Shipment Delivery

Ocean freight booking, Bill of Lading generation, and real-time cargo updates until port of destination clearance.

division 02

Global Sourcing & Procurement

Serving as your strategic global procurement partner to manage complexity and stabilize supply chain inputs.

We provide professional end-to-end procurement solutions for businesses requiring secure supply lines. Our strategic sourcing framework is engineered to manage quality risks, simplify supplier vetting, and oversee production standards directly.

By acting as an on-the-ground procurement desk, we manage everything from manufacturer sourcing and specification alignment to pre-shipment inspections and port logistics. We do not engage in casual transactional buying; instead, we build long-term supply agreements that safeguard our clients' resource stability.
