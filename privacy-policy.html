Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains how Alteon Exports Ltd collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information.

Section 01

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you use our website, fill in contact or enquiry forms, or communicate with our team. This may include:

  • Your full name and job title or company name
  • Business email address and phone number
  • Country of operation and shipping destination
  • Commodity requirements and trade volumes specified in enquiries
  • Any other information you voluntarily submit through our contact or quote forms

We may also collect certain technical data automatically when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on site. This data is used in aggregate form for analytics purposes only.

Section 02

How We Use Your Information

Alteon Exports Ltd uses the information we collect solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to your enquiries and providing requested quotations
  • Processing and fulfilling trade and procurement requests
  • Communicating about shipment status, documentation, and logistics updates
  • Improving our website and the quality of our service offering
  • Meeting legal or regulatory obligations under Nigerian and international trade law

We do not use your personal data for unsolicited marketing or sell it to advertising networks.

Section 03

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With trusted logistics partners, freight forwarders, or customs agents as required to fulfil your shipment
  • With third-party laboratory or inspection bodies (e.g., SGS, Bureau Veritas) to conduct quality assays on your behalf
  • When required by Nigerian government authorities, NEPC, or international regulatory bodies
  • With professional advisers such as lawyers or accountants under strict confidentiality obligations

Any third party engaged by Alteon Exports Ltd is required to handle your data in compliance with applicable data protection standards.

Section 04

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as it is necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Trade and transaction records may be retained for up to seven (7) years to meet legal, financial, and export compliance obligations. Enquiry data from prospective clients who do not proceed to a transaction is typically retained for up to twelve (12) months.

Section 05

Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies required for basic functionality (e.g., navigation and form submission). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Any analytics tools used operate in anonymised, aggregate mode and do not identify individual visitors. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though this may affect certain features of the site.

Section 06

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • The right to request erasure of your data where there is no lawful basis for retention
  • The right to object to or restrict certain types of processing
  • The right to data portability in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 10 below. We aim to respond to all valid requests within 30 days.

Section 07

Security

We take the security of your information seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted email communication, restricted access controls, and secure data storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 08

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external partner sites, certification bodies, or regulatory authorities. These websites operate under their own privacy policies, and Alteon Exports Ltd assumes no responsibility for the content or practices of any third-party site. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

Section 09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operations, services, or legal obligations. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
